Goals by spell

The same goals, grouped. The 15-minute blocks are comparable with each other. The 45+ and 90+ rows are only the added minutes, and the later ones are not played in every match — a short bar there does not mean those minutes were quiet.

How the minutes were read. The source lists goal minutes in the order they happened. First-half stoppage keeps counting past 45, then the second half starts again at 46, so the same number can mean two different moments. Each match was split with its half-time score: the first goals, up to the half-time total, are the first half. A first-half 47 is shown as 45+2. A second-half 47 stays 47. Minutes past 90 are shown as 90+1, 90+2, and so on. The latest goal in these seasons is 90+13.
